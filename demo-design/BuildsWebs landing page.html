Google Business Profile setup and management

Your Google Business Profile is what appears in map results when people search for you. We set it up, verify it and keep it active, so it shows accurate details and brings calls and messages.

Ask about Google Business Profile on WhatsAppSee all services

What is included

Setup and verification

We create or claim your profile and complete Google’s verification.

Categories and services

The right primary category, and your services listed accurately.

Photos and posts

Real photos and regular updates that show the business is active.

Reviews and questions

Replies to reviews and answers to common questions.

Monthly insights

We review calls, direction requests and website clicks, and act on them.

Who this is for

Businesses with a shop, clinic or office, and service businesses that travel to customers, who want to be found in map results.

Common questions

Does Google charge for a business profile?

No. The profile is free. You pay us for the setup and ongoing work, not for the listing.

What if my profile gets suspended?

Suspensions usually come from guideline issues such as address or category problems. We find the cause and submit a reinstatement request, though the decision is Google’s.

Can I have a profile without a shop?

Yes. A service-area business can hide its address and list the areas it serves.
